OPENHEALTH PATHMNIST FEDERATION
EXEMPLAR MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

Status: Proof-of-concept constitutional specification
Federation: Hospital A–Hospital B PathMNIST Federation
Policy identifier: POL-OPENHEALTH-PATHMNIST-AB

1. FOUNDING MEMBERS

[AB-MEMBER-01] The founding members of the federation are:

* Hospital A, represented by [Director A name]
* Hospital B, represented by [Director B name]

[AB-MEMBER-02] Each hospital remains institutionally independent and retains responsibility for its personnel, systems, credentials, delegated authorities, and local data processing.

2. FEDERATION PURPOSE

[AB-PURPOSE-01] The federation is constituted to train and use a shared PathMNIST colon-pathology classification model without transferring raw clinical images between members.

[AB-PURPOSE-02] Only actions performed for federated training, evaluation, and approved model query are within the purpose of this federation.

3. CONTRIBUTION RIGHTS

[AB-CONTRIB-A-01] Hospital A may contribute training observations associated with:

* adipose
* background
* debris

[AB-CONTRIB-A-02] Hospital A may not contribute observations associated with pathology classes outside this declared contribution scope.

[AB-CONTRIB-B-01] Hospital B may contribute training observations associated with:

* lymphocytes
* mucus
* smooth muscle

[AB-CONTRIB-B-02] Hospital B may not contribute observations associated with pathology classes outside this declared contribution scope.

4. QUERY RIGHTS

[AB-QUERY-01] Query authority is independent from contribution authority.

[AB-QUERY-02] A token may authorize queries over all or only a subset of the pathology classes available within the federation.

[AB-QUERY-A-01] Hospital A may issue tokens authorizing queries over pathology classes approved for Hospital A under the federation policy.

[AB-QUERY-B-01] Hospital B may issue tokens authorizing queries over pathology classes approved for Hospital B under the federation policy.

[AB-QUERY-03] No token may authorize queries over a pathology class not covered by the active federation envelope.

5. DELEGATION

[AB-DELEGATE-01] A hospital administrator may delegate bounded token-issuing authority to an identified team leader.

[AB-DELEGATE-02] A delegated token must remain a strict subset of the delegating authority with respect to:

* permitted operations
* query scope
* contribution scope
* federation envelope
* session
* validity period

[AB-DELEGATE-03] Delegation must not permit privilege amplification.

[AB-DELEGATE-04] The delegating hospital remains accountable for tokens issued under its delegated authority.

6. ADMISSION AND SESSION BINDING

[AB-SESSION-01] Participation is valid only within an active federation envelope.

[AB-SESSION-02] Session-bound authority expires when the named session ends or when its token expires, whichever occurs first.

[AB-SESSION-03] Authority granted for one envelope or session must not be reused in another envelope or session.

7. ACCOUNTABILITY AND EVIDENCE

[AB-EVIDENCE-01] Every admission decision must produce verifiable evidence identifying:

* the admitted subject
* the issuing organization
* the applicable federation envelope
* the session
* the granted capabilities
* the applicable scopes
* the policy version
* the validity period

[AB-EVIDENCE-02] Every delegated token must preserve an auditable chain to the accountable hospital.

8. POLICY FORMALIZATION RULE

[AB-FORMAL-01] A rule may be included in policy.json only when it can be expressed as a constitutional statement jointly agreed by the hospital directors.

[AB-FORMAL-02] Infrastructure implementation details, including service registration, container deployment, backend discovery, and local training execution, are not constitutional rules unless the members explicitly agree to govern them at the federation boundary.

[AB-FORMAL-03] Each machine-readable rule in policy.json must reference the corresponding rule identifier in this Memorandum of Understanding.
