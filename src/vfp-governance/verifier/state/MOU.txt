MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
OPENHEALTH PATHMNIST A+B RESEARCH COLLABORATION
Proof-of-Concept Constitutional Agreement

Document ID: MOU-OPENHEALTH-PATHMNIST-AB
Version: 2.5-draft
Status: Research proof of concept; not for clinical use
Parties: Hospital A and Hospital B
Referenced dataset: PathMNIST
Research collaboration: OpenHealth PathMNIST A+B


1. PURPOSE AND STATUS

AB-PURPOSE-01
Hospital A and Hospital B agree to conduct a joint research activity using federated learning on the PathMNIST dataset.

AB-PURPOSE-02
The purpose of the collaboration is to study how hospitals may contribute to a shared model while retaining control of their local data, limiting the scope of the collaboration, and governing access to the resulting model.

AB-PURPOSE-03
This collaboration is a technical and organizational proof of concept. Its model, predictions, metrics, and decisions are not validated for diagnosis, treatment, patient management, or any other clinical use.

AB-PURPOSE-04
This Memorandum is the human-readable statement of the constitutional rules agreed by the participating hospitals. A structured machine-readable constitution and an executable policy may be derived from it for implementation and testing.


2. PARTICIPATING HOSPITALS

AB-MEMBER-01
Hospital A and Hospital B are the founding participants in the OpenHealth PathMNIST A+B research collaboration.

AB-MEMBER-02
Each hospital remains responsible for its own local data, local research environment, organizational credentials, and the persons or services acting under its authority.

AB-MEMBER-03
Participation in the collaboration does not create an unrestricted right to train, evaluate, query, copy, export, administer, or possess the shared model.

AB-MEMBER-04
Any activity not explicitly authorized by this agreement or by a valid authorization derived from it shall be refused.


3. PATHMNIST RESEARCH SCOPE

AB-LABEL-01
The collaboration uses the following PathMNIST tissue classes:

  0  adipose
  1  background
  2  debris
  3  lymphocytes
  4  mucus
  5  smooth muscle
  6  normal colon mucosa
  7  cancer-associated stroma
  8  colorectal adenocarcinoma epithelium

AB-LABEL-02
For governance and presentation, adipose, debris, lymphocytes, mucus, smooth muscle, and normal colon mucosa are designated OTHER TISSUE CLASSES.

AB-LABEL-03
Cancer-associated stroma and colorectal adenocarcinoma epithelium are designated CANCER-ASSOCIATED TISSUE CLASSES.

AB-LABEL-04
Cancer-associated stroma is tumor-associated stromal tissue. Colorectal adenocarcinoma epithelium is the malignant epithelial class. The collective designation “cancer-associated” shall not erase this distinction.

AB-LABEL-05
Background is designated the RESERVED TISSUE CLASS for this collaboration.


4. TRAINING AND EVALUATION

AB-TRAIN-01
The authorized training scope comprises all PathMNIST tissue classes except background.

AB-TRAIN-02
background shall be removed from the local training data before federated training begins.

AB-TRAIN-03
background shall not be included in the governed evaluation results used to describe the research collaboration.

AB-TRAIN-04
The exact allocation of samples, random seeds, optimization method, number of rounds, computing equipment, and similar implementation choices are operational research parameters rather than constitutional rights.

AB-CONTRIB-01
Each hospital may contribute only through the research activity, purpose, tissue scope, and authorization context approved for that collaboration.

AB-CONTRIB-02
Raw PathMNIST images or patient-level data shall not be transferred from one hospital to another as part of the federated training process.

AB-CONTRIB-03
Only approved model updates, approved evaluation information, and required research evidence may leave a hospital’s local execution environment.


5. SHARED MODEL CUSTODY

AB-CUSTODY-01
The aggregated federated model is a protected shared research asset.

AB-CUSTODY-02
The model shall be stored and operated within a protected environment controlled on behalf of the collaboration.

AB-CUSTODY-03
A hospital’s contribution to training does not grant that hospital direct possession of, filesystem access to, download rights for, or unrestricted execution rights over the aggregated model.

AB-CUSTODY-04
The model may be loaded inside the protected environment for technical validation and maintenance. Such internal access does not create an external access right.

AB-CUSTODY-05
External predictions or model-derived results shall be obtained only through an approved service that applies the agreed authorization rules.

AB-CUSTODY-06
Contribution rights, custody of the shared model, and rights to query the model are separate authorities. Possession of one shall not imply possession of the others.


6. RESEARCH ROLES AND QUERY SCOPE

AB-QUERY-01
The tissue classes used during training and the tissue classes available to a particular researcher or research role at query time are separate matters.

AB-QUERY-02
A tissue class may be included in federated training while remaining unavailable to a particular researcher, team, hospital, or research role.

AB-QUERY-03
A researcher authorized as an OTHER TISSUE READER may query only the other-tissue classes explicitly granted to that role.

AB-QUERY-04
For the minimal proof-of-concept case, an OTHER TISSUE READER may be authorized to query mucus, normal colon mucosa, and lymphocytes.

AB-QUERY-05
Cancer-associated stroma and colorectal adenocarcinoma epithelium require a separate CANCER-ASSOCIATED TISSUE READER authorization.

AB-QUERY-06
The presence of a tissue class in the trained model does not by itself create a right to query that class.

AB-QUERY-07
Any request that includes background shall be refused because background is outside the authorized learning and query scope of the collaboration.

AB-QUERY-08
A request containing both authorized and unauthorized tissue classes shall be refused as submitted rather than silently reduced or rewritten.


6A. PARTICIPATION GRADES AND SPONSORSHIP

AB-GRADE-01
The collaboration may admit additional participants only under participation grades declared by the constitutional agreement and executable policy in force when the collaboration envelope is approved.

AB-GRADE-02
Admission of a new actor under an already declared participation grade does not change the constitutional rules of the collaboration.

AB-GRADE-03
Introduction of a new participation grade is a constitutional change and requires corresponding re-approval of the collaboration.

AB-GUEST-01
A founding hospital may sponsor a human guest contributor under an explicitly declared guest-contributor grade.

AB-GUEST-02
A guest contributor may participate only in the bounded federated-training activity, purpose, scope, collaboration, and model run for which the guest was admitted.

AB-GUEST-03
Guest contribution does not create founding membership, quorum rights, model custody rights, model-query rights, or any other authority not separately granted.

AB-GUEST-04
A guest authorization shall explicitly identify its sponsoring founding hospital. The GUEST CONTRIBUTOR grade requires exactly one sponsoring founding hospital; sponsorship shall not be inferred from the identity of the issuer alone.

AB-GUEST-05
The institution associated with a guest contribution remains the provenance source of the contributed data. Sponsorship does not transfer custody or ownership of that data to the sponsoring hospital.

AB-AGENT-01
The collaboration may admit a non-human computational participant only under an explicitly declared participation grade and explicit sponsorship by accountable founding hospitals. The BOUNDED AGENT PARTICIPANT grade requires at least two sponsoring founding hospitals.

AB-AGENT-02
A non-human computational participant does not acquire founding membership, quorum rights, model custody rights, issuer authority, or any other federation authority not explicitly granted by its admission. Its accountability path shall terminate in the human or legal parties that sponsor its admission.

AB-AGENT-03
Admission grants a non-human computational participant only the bounded authority represented by its admitted capability. Runtime functions, available tools, execution strategy, or technical reachability shall not enlarge that authority.

AB-AGENT-04
A bounded agent participant may be authorized to perform narrowly scoped model inference and a separately authorized rebind operation without receiving unrestricted model-query or source-consumption authority.

AB-AGENT-05
A rebind operation transforms a restricted source result into a derivative representation according to federation policy. The permitted transformation shall be established by federation governance and shall not be determined by the computational participant as an independent judgement that the source result is safe to release.

AB-AGENT-06
Source consumption and derivative consumption are separate governed relations. Authorization to consume a derivative representation shall not grant authority to consume the corresponding unrestricted source result, and shall be evaluated under a separate capability held by the recipient.

7. AUTHORIZATION CONTEXT

AB-CONTEXT-01
Every authorization shall be valid only for the particular approved research collaboration for which it was issued.

AB-CONTEXT-02
An authorization issued for one collaboration shall not be reused in another collaboration, even when the researcher, hospital, role, purpose, and requested tissue classes are otherwise the same.

AB-CONTEXT-03
Several approved collaborations may exist concurrently. Each authorization shall remain bound to its own collaboration.

AB-CONTEXT-04
The implementation shall preserve this contextual binding in a form that can be independently verified when a request is evaluated.

AB-CONTEXT-05
The implementation may assign a technical identifier to each approved collaboration. That identifier is an implementation detail and does not alter the institutional meaning of this rule.


8. AUTHORIZED ISSUERS AND CAPABILITIES

AB-ISSUER-01
Only an issuer acting under the authority of Hospital A or Hospital B may create an authorization for this collaboration.

AB-ISSUER-02
Each issuer may create only the research roles and capabilities that its hospital is authorized to grant.

AB-ISSUER-03
An issuer shall not grant a broader action, purpose, tissue scope, collaboration context, audience, or validity period than its own authority permits.

AB-ISSUER-04
No issuer may grant access to background.

AB-ISSUER-05
The implementation shall preserve traceability between each research role, the corresponding capability, and the clauses of this Memorandum from which that capability is derived.


9. HOLDER IDENTITY AND PROOF OF AUTHORITY

AB-HOLDER-01
Every authorization shall identify or securely bind the researcher, service, or other holder to whom it is granted.

AB-HOLDER-02
The presenter of an authorization shall provide evidence that it is the legitimate holder.

AB-HOLDER-03
A request presented by a different holder from the one bound to the authorization shall be refused.

AB-HOLDER-04
A human-readable name, organizational role, bearer assertion, or possession of an authorization string alone is insufficient proof of legitimate authority.


10. DELEGATION

AB-DELEGATE-01
A hospital administrator may delegate limited authorization authority to an approved subordinate role only when such delegation is expressly permitted by the collaboration’s constitution.

AB-DELEGATE-02
A delegated authorization shall remain a strict subset of the parent authority.

AB-DELEGATE-03
Delegation shall not broaden the permitted actions, purposes, tissue classes, collaboration context, audience, or validity period.

AB-DELEGATE-04
A delegated authorization shall expire no later than the parent authorization.

AB-DELEGATE-05
Delegation shall not permit privilege amplification. The sponsoring hospital shall remain accountable for the delegated authority.


11. EVIDENCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY

AB-EVIDENCE-01
The collaboration shall retain sufficient evidence to determine who issued an authorization, to whom it was granted, which sponsoring organizations were asserted when sponsorship is required, what it permitted, for which collaboration it was valid, and for how long.

AB-EVIDENCE-02
Each evaluated request shall produce an auditable decision indicating whether it was allowed or refused and the reason for that decision.

AB-EVIDENCE-03
Decision evidence shall be linked, where appropriate, to the corresponding collaboration and model run.

AB-EVIDENCE-04
Private keys, raw credentials, and other secrets shall not be included in evidence exposed to researchers or user interfaces.

AB-EVIDENCE-05
The evidence shall be sufficient to distinguish at least:

  authorization granted
  reserved tissue requested
  requested scope exceeded
  authorization used in the wrong collaboration
  holder identity mismatch
  invalid or expired authorization


AB-EVIDENCE-06
An admission decision shall preserve sufficient information to identify the participation grade or capability profile under which the participant was admitted and the verified sponsoring organizations when sponsorship is required.

AB-EVIDENCE-07
For federated training, one admission decision may bind a participant to one bounded model run. Internal protocol exchanges within that run inherit the admission and do not require independent admission decisions.

AB-EVIDENCE-08
Interactive model use and other independently governed operations shall be evaluated at their declared operation boundary.


12. CHANGE CONTROL

AB-CHANGE-01
A change to the participating hospitals, participation grades, sponsorship rules, reserved tissue classes, model custody, research roles, authorization scope, contextual binding, issuer authority, or delegation rules is a constitutional change.

AB-CHANGE-02
A constitutional change shall require corresponding updates to this Memorandum and to the machine-readable constitution and executable policy derived from it.

AB-CHANGE-03
A change limited to implementation detail or operational training parameters may be recorded in run configuration and evidence without amending this Memorandum, provided it does not alter an agreed governance boundary.

AB-CHANGE-04
Each derived machine-readable artifact shall identify the version of this Memorandum from which it was produced.


13. AGREEMENT

For this proof-of-concept exemplar, the representatives of Hospital A and Hospital B acknowledge that the OpenHealth PathMNIST A+B research collaboration operates according to the governance boundaries stated above.
